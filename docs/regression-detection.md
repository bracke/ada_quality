# Regression detection

Regression kinds are new violation, worsened metric, increased severity, failed gate, lost evidence, and policy-induced change. Direction is explicit per numeric indicator: TST-02 registration counts and TST-03 coverage worsen when they fall; violation, complexity, documentation-warning, and artifact counts worsen when they rise; indicators without a defined numeric direction are not trended. Metric deterioration is reported even when both assessments remain in violation. A pass-to-violation on build/test/release/proof gates is a failed gate. Toolchain-incompatible or incomplete runs are not compared and are reported as unavailable rather than clean.

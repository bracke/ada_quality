# Coverage

TST-03 accepts only complete, source-bound `gcov-production-lines-v2` evidence. The driver enumerates units from `src` before inspecting coverage files and fails if any eligible unit lacks its `<stem>.gcno` instrumentation. Bodies and specifications are reconciled against per-file gcov records; gcov's final aggregate line is excluded from per-source parsing. The self-hosting `ada_quality_coverage` driver is the one explicit exclusion because it owns the active coverage files and is verification tooling rather than a shipped Alire executable. Evidence records that exclusion. The gate requires nonzero eligible executable lines and compares exact integers against the policy basis points.
